### Envato Market / CodeCanyon
- Product is licensed under Envato Market Regular/Extended License. Installation requires an Envato purchase code, a personal token with **Verify Purchases** permission, and the numeric `ENVATO_ITEM_ID`.
- License verification runs against Envato Market API v3 during installation and can be re-run from Admin → License when migrating domains.
